# Example: Funding round trigger for a RevOps tooling ICP

## Scenario

You sell a revenue operations data platform to Series A-B SaaS companies with 100-500 employees. Your ICP buyer is a VP of Revenue Operations or Director of Sales Operations. The trigger that matters most is a funding round: it signals budget, growth pressure, and a leadership team that now has to professionalize their GTM stack.

---

## How the trigger surfaced

**Monday, 9:14 AM.** Funded Today newsletter (daily digest) lands in your inbox. One entry:

> "Acme Co raised a $20M Series B led by Andreessen Horowitz. Acme provides logistics software for mid-market e-commerce companies."

Two minutes later, Crunchbase sends a saved-search alert for "Series B rounds, SaaS, 101-500 employees."

Same company. Same round.

LinkedIn's algorithm surfaces a post from Acme's CEO an hour later announcing the raise and tagging a16z's deal lead.

You now have the event confirmed from three independent sources.

---

## Relevance check

Before acting, verify Acme fits the ICP:

- **Firmographics:** 280 employees (LinkedIn headcount), SaaS, Series B confirmed - within target range. Check.
- **Buyer role exists:** Search LinkedIn People at Acme for "Revenue Operations" or "Sales Operations." Result: one Director of Revenue Operations, hired 8 months ago. Check.
- **Round size and investor:** $20M Series B with a16z is a signal of real operational ambition, not just a bridge. Andreessen has a pattern of pushing portfolio companies to build RevOps infrastructure early. Check.
- **Not a disqualifier:** Not a consultancy, not pre-Series A, not above 500 employees, not already a customer (checked CRM). No red flags.

Acme passes the relevance check.

---

## The window

A funding announcement creates a 2-5 business day window where the signal is fresh. After that:
- The team is heads-down executing on what they committed to investors
- Inbound congratulations messages start to pile up and yours blends in
- The VP of RevOps shifts from "we just raised, what do we need" to "I'm already 3 weeks into my new projects"

This is Tuesday. Send Tuesday or Wednesday. Not Friday.

---

## The action

The trigger gives you a specific, observable reason to reach out that isn't about you. The opener anchors on the event, connects it to a specific operational challenge that typically follows a round of this size, and asks one concrete question.

**Opener structure (for `icebreaker` to draft):**

```
${TRIGGER_DETAIL}: "Congratulations on the Series B - $20M with a16z is a real signal."

${CONNECTION_TO_PAIN}: "Post-Series B RevOps teams at companies your size typically hit the same wall: the spreadsheet-and-Salesforce stack that worked at $5M ARR starts breaking at $15M+, right when the board is watching pipeline metrics most closely."

${OFFER_HOOK}: "[One sentence on what you do and the specific outcome it delivers - pulled from your offer-clarifier output.]"

${CTA}: "Worth 20 minutes to show you what that looks like at your stage?"
```

30-45 words in the actual message. The structure above is the brief; `icebreaker` writes the final copy.

---

Pass to `icebreaker` with:

`icebreaker` will draft three openers from distinct angles - Trigger-based will be the natural lead given the freshness of the event.
